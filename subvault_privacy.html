SubVault

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Overview

SubVault ("the App") is a private, offline-first subscription and promotional deal tracker.

Your privacy is fundamental to how SubVault is built: all data you enter stays exclusively on your device.

Data Collection and Sharing

SubVault does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or financial data.

  • No Internet Access: The App does not request Android internet or network permissions and never communicates with external servers.
  • No Accounts or Tracking: There is no sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics, and no advertising SDKs.
  • On-Device Storage Only: Your subscriptions, renewal dates, promo deals, alert history, and settings are stored strictly in your device's local storage (Room SQLite database and Android DataStore).
  • Security Features: If you set an App PIN, it is stored locally as a salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hash. If you enable biometric or device-credential unlock, authentication is handled directly by Android; SubVault never receives or stores biometric data.
  • Backups: If you choose to export a JSON backup, the file is created only when you initiate it and saved to the location you pick via Android's system file picker.

Permissions Used

SubVault requests only two minimal permissions:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, optional): Used solely to show local on-device reminders for upcoming promotional deal expirations and price-hike alerts.
  • Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used solely to restore your scheduled local reminders after a device restart or app update.

Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data at all times. You can delete individual subscriptions, erase all stored records immediately using Settings → Reset All Data, or remove all app data by uninstalling SubVault.

Children's Privacy

SubVault does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
